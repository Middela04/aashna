About the App

Why Khushii exists and how it supports growth between sessions.

Khushii's purpose

Khushii is a free mental health learning and growth platform designed to support individuals and, when helpful, the providers who work with them. The goal is not to replace therapy, but to give people a space to practice healthier habits, build insight, and stay connected to their progress between appointments.

Why this matters in South Asian communities

In many South Asian communities, mental health can still carry stigma, silence, and internalized shame. People may feel pressure to look strong, stay quiet, or treat emotional pain as something to hide instead of something to understand. That can make it harder to ask for help, name stress early, or build supportive routines.

What the app is meant to do

Khushii is meant to help people learn more about their emotional patterns, develop better habits, and practice healthier ways of thinking when they are not sitting directly in front of their provider. Through lessons, reflections, coping tools, journaling, and small daily practices, the app encourages steady growth over time instead of waiting for change to happen only during a session.

How providers can use it

Khushii is designed to complement—not replace—professional mental health care. Between sessions, users can practice coping skills, reflect through guided journaling, complete short psychoeducational lessons, and build healthy habits. Providers may choose to recommend Khushii as an optional companion resource that helps clients stay engaged with the work of therapy and arrive at sessions with greater self-awareness and a clearer picture of their progress.

What Khushii is not

Khushii is not a crisis service, diagnosis tool, or replacement for professional care. It is a supportive platform for learning, self-reflection, and practicing new mental health habits in everyday life.
