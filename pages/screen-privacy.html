Privacy Policy

How Aashna stores and uses account and wellness progress data.

Last updated: July 9, 2026

What Aashna stores

Aashna stores the account details you provide, including your email address, display name, session information, app settings, lesson progress, journal entries, affirmations, toolkit items, check-ins, and other profile data needed to keep your experience consistent across devices.

How data is used

Your data is used to authenticate your account, restore your saved progress, personalize your experience inside the app, and support basic app operations such as profile loading, progress tracking, and login sessions.

How data is stored

Aashna stores account and profile data on its backend database. Session cookies are used to keep you signed in securely. Production deployments are expected to use HTTPS and secure cookie settings.

What Aashna does not promise

Aashna is not a clinical records system, crisis service, or emergency response tool. Do not use the app to store information that you would only want handled inside a formal medical record.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. If you need help with your account or want to request support related to stored data, use the Support page in the app and contact the organization through the channels listed there.
