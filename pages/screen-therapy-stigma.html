Therapy Stigma Explainer
Plain-language answers for common fears about therapy.
Therapy does not mean I am broken.

Therapy is support for patterns, stress, grief, relationships, and emotions. You do not have to be in crisis to deserve help.

Talking to someone outside the family is not betrayal.

A therapist is not there to judge your family. They help you understand your experience and choose healthier responses.

Culturally competent care matters.

You can ask a therapist if they understand immigration, bicultural identity, family expectations, faith, caste, language, or stigma.

What can I say to my family?

"I am getting support so I can handle stress in a healthier way. This is private, but I want you to know I am taking care of myself."

Questions to ask a therapist

Have you worked with South Asian clients? How do you approach family pressure? Do you offer sliding scale fees? What would our first few sessions look like?
