Terms of Use

The basic rules and limits for using Khushii responsibly.

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Purpose of the app

Khushii is a mental wellness support app designed to offer reflection tools, grounding exercises, learning modules, and progress tracking. It is not a replacement for therapy, emergency services, diagnosis, or crisis intervention.

Eligibility and account use

You are responsible for the accuracy of the account information you provide and for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. Do not share your account in ways that could expose your saved data to other people.

Acceptable use

You agree to use Khushii only for lawful, personal, and supportive purposes. You must not use the service to abuse, disrupt, reverse engineer, or interfere with the experience of other users or with the operation of the app.

Health and safety disclaimer

If you are in crisis or immediate danger, contact local emergency services or call or text 988 in the United States. Khushii may offer supportive content, but it does not provide medical advice or emergency intervention.

Service availability

Khushii may change, pause, or update features over time. Access may occasionally be interrupted by maintenance, deployment changes, third-party outages, or other technical issues.
