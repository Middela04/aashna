Support

Help for account access, bugs, questions, and feedback.

What support covers

Use support for account access issues, questions about saved progress, problems signing in, unexpected app behavior, or general feedback about the product experience.

How to reach the team

Aashna was developed by MHISA. For support and organization contact details, visit the MHISA website and use the published contact channels there.

Visit mhisa.org ↗

When to seek immediate help instead

Support is not a crisis line. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent mental health help, call emergency services or call or text 988 in the United States.

What to include in a support request

Include the device you used, whether you signed in with email/password or Google, what you were trying to do, and what went wrong. That will make it much easier to diagnose the issue quickly.
